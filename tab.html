name of project: Gummie

gummie

May 31st 2023

UI/UX Study, Project

visit full website

My role for this particular project was as a designer and researcher, focusing on the exploration of the brutalism web design trend of 2021, a style that emphasizes simplicity and accessibility. The goal was to showcase how brutalism, with its stark, minimalistic approach and focus on raw functionality, could enhance user accessibility and engagement without sacrificing aesthetic appeal. Undertaken in August 2022, this project presented the challenge of balancing brutalist principles with user-friendly design, ensuring that the website remained accessible and appealing to all users, including beginners in web design and coding. Our methodology involved thorough research and analysis of existing brutalist designs, followed by the application of color theory, grid layouts, and typography to maintain both accessibility and visual interest. Using tools like Figma for wireframing, Coolors for color schemes, and various open-source resources, we developed a prototype that adhered to brutalism while pushing the boundaries of traditional web accessibility standards. This brief, intensive project concluded that brutalism and accessibility could indeed go hand in hand, leading to innovative design solutions that cater to a wide range of users.

name of project: Encounter Me

ENCOUNTER ME

SPRING 22

Responsive Web Design, Project

visit full website

We are a campus based direct food delivery service. Buildt/Ran by students for students, staff, and members of the University. There's no denying that the campus of University at Albany is fairly large. And for students who dorm further away from the campus center/ dining halls, getting food is a hasel. And even with conventional food delivery services such as dominos, Uber, Grubhub, postmates etc. Getting delivery is not a piece of cake. This is an architectural demosite/ trail run for campus based delivery service in just 5 webpages. Not only do hungry students get adequate service, but students in need of employment can have the opportunity to work with flexibility. The goal of the site is to provide users with safe delivery services on demand options.
